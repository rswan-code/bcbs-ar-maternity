Executive View

In its first three months, Pyx Health has enrolled 291 ARHOME pregnant and postpartum members — establishing EPDS baselines averaging 7.2 (18–24 cohort: 8.7, elevated), documenting 88% SDoH need driven by economic instability, and generating the member-level data ABCBS's Special Delivery program needs but cannot produce from provider encounters alone.

Pyx has outreached to 1,071 members and enrolled 291 through Month 3. The program picture is sharpening every month: the members Pyx is finding have an average of 3.2 documented SDoH needs, an average EPDS of 7.2, and 88% are food-insecure — yet they are opting in and staying engaged.

ARKANSAS CONTEXT — WHY THIS MATTERS

4th highest preterm birth rate · high maternal mortality · rural access gaps — the conditions that make Pyx's engagement model non-optional

Arkansas ranks among the worst states in the country for maternal health outcomes. High preterm birth rates, significant rural transportation barriers, and limited prenatal provider access in many counties mean that reaching a pregnant ARHOME member with a standard reminder call is not enough. Pyx's model — food box milestone incentives + human CSC support + EPDS screening + SDoH navigation — is designed specifically for the conditions Arkansas members face. The program doesn't ask members to engage with a portal. It calls them, meets them where they are, and rewards care actions with something they need. That's why it works where standard outreach doesn't.

The Premise
Food is not the incentive — it is the intervention
Standard incentive programs pair a health action with a gift card or points. The Motivate program is built on a different premise: when the #1 documented barrier is food insecurity, putting food at the door in response to a care action isn't incentive theory — it is direct barrier removal. A member who completes an EPDS screening or attests a prenatal visit doesn't receive a thank-you message. She receives fresh produce or pantry staples delivered to her home that week, with no application, no waitlist, and no pickup required.
THE CASE FOR FOOD-FIRST DESIGN

81% of members document food insecurity — it's the #1 barrier

Every enrolled member completes a PRAPARE social needs assessment. 81% report food insecurity as a current, active need. This isn't a background risk factor — it's the household reality in which prenatal appointments, medication adherence, and postpartum visits compete with buying groceries. Motivate responds to that reality directly: complete the care action, get food. The box is both the barrier response and the engagement signal.

WHY THIS BUILDS LASTING TRUST

Box arrives → next call answered → next screening completed

When a member receives a produce box the same week she completed her EPDS screening, the message is concrete: Pyx follows through. That trust compounds. The member who got food after her first prenatal visit is more likely to pick up the postpartum visit reminder. Motivate is not just activation — it's the relationship infrastructure that makes every subsequent touchpoint easier.
